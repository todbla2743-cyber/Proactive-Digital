Todd Blackwell Jr. has spent his career on the operating side of real businesses, B2B sales, multi-location operations, and building a company from nothing alongside his wife Robbyn. He knows what it actually takes to run a small business, because he has done it.

Read the story behind Proactive Digital

Before Proactive Digital, there was a vending route, a stack of used machines, and a belief that regular people could build something real if they just had the right tools and the courage to start. Armed with a B.S. in Business Administration and real-world experience, he built a vending operation from the ground up with hustle, strategy, and a willingness to figure things out in real time.

Proactive Digital was born from that same mindset. A business's digital presence should make it easier to earn trust, communicate value, and move customers forward. That means more than a polished homepage — it means clear strategy, thoughtful design, and practical systems that fit how the business actually operates. Every project gets direct founder involvement and decisions grounded in business outcomes.
