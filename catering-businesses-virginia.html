Common questions

A practical fit for your business.

Do you serve all of Virginia?

Yes. Proactive Digital is based in Chesterfield and works with food businesses across Virginia, including Northern Virginia, Hampton Roads, Central Virginia, the Shenandoah Valley, and Southwest Virginia. Discovery and project reviews can be handled remotely.

Do I need to replace my current website?

We review what you already use before recommending changes. A focused landing page, improved form, or better handoff may be enough. Platform constraints and integrations are checked during scoping.

Will AI quote prices or confirm bookings for me?

Your team controls final prices, availability, and customer commitments. Where helpful, AI can assist with summaries and drafts for review. The exact workflow is agreed before implementation.

What if my business needs more inquiries first?

We can start with your message, service pages, Google presence, and inquiry pathway. An elaborate follow-up system is not useful if the first priority is helping suitable customers find you.
