The challenge

A foundation for the next chapter

Author and founder Tamesha McRae needed a publishing website that reflected her purpose, introduced Rooted in Him, and made it easy for readers to discover her work and connect.

The launch also required coordination across the domain, website hosting, and business email so the brand could move forward with a connected online presence.

The approach

Let the reader's journey lead

We created an editorial experience in cream, soft lavender, plum, and gold. The book and author lead the story, with clear routes to book details, events, inquiries, and community updates.

Six connected pages give each part of the publishing brand room to grow: Home, Books, About, Author, Events, and Contact.

What we delivered

A website backed by launch support.

The reader experience

  • Custom book and author presentation, including front and back cover views.
  • A direct Amazon purchase path for Rooted in Him.
  • Contact and email signup forms with consent.
  • Event details and responsive navigation across devices.

Support beyond the pages

  • Domain transition and website routing coordination.
  • Connection of the main domain and www address with HTTPS.
  • Google Workspace business email activation and login handoff.
  • Email authentication records to support trusted delivery.
The outcome

A live platform for books, readers, and future growth.

MRA Legacy Publishing now has a live branded website and active business email. Readers can explore the book, follow its Amazon purchase link, view event information, and contact the author. Proactive Digital's work connected the visual experience with the practical setup behind the launch.
