A FEW PRACTICAL QUESTIONS

Built around
your starting point.

What if I already have a website?+

We can start by reviewing what you already have: the mobile experience, project examples, business details, estimate process, and the path from inquiry to follow-up. The first proposal can focus on the most useful improvements.

Can we start small?+

Yes. Your first step might be a focused website refresh, a better estimate form, or an organized project gallery. We agree on a manageable scope and add other systems when they make sense.

What does ongoing support include?+

The support agreement defines it. Options can include project updates, form checks, content changes, visibility improvements, and a review of inquiry activity with practical next steps.

Will I own my website and accounts?+

Client ownership is central to Proactive Digital. Your proposal explains the website deliverables, account access, third-party services, and any ongoing fees so responsibilities are clear before work starts.
