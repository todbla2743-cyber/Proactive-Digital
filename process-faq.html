Questions, Answered

What most people ask
before they start.

How long does it take?
Timelines depend on scope. Focused website projects can move quickly, while multi-page builds, content-heavy work, and integrated business systems need more planning and testing. Your proposal will include a clear target timeline before work begins.
Do I own my website?
Yes. 100 percent. The site, the domain, the content, all yours. No hostage situations, no platform lock-in.
What if I need changes after launch?
You can stay on an ongoing support plan or request one-time improvements as needed. The goal is to give you reliable support without forcing a larger engagement than your business needs.
What do you need from me to start?
Start with your goals. A logo, photos, current website, service list, or examples you like are helpful, but we can guide the process and identify what is actually needed during discovery.
What does it cost?
Every project is scoped to the business need. After a consultation, you receive a clear proposal with the recommended solution, scope, timeline, investment, and payment structure before work begins.
Can you help with Google and getting found?
Yes. Search structure, metadata, local visibility, and Google Business guidance can be included based on the engagement. We focus on a strong foundation and practical improvements rather than promising overnight rankings.
